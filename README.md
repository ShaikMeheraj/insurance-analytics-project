# Insurance Sales Analytics Dashboard
Insurance Sales Analytics Dashboard | Premium, Revenue, Renewals and Cross-Sell Insights

## Project Overview
Developed an end-to-end Insurance Sales Analytics dashboard using SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau to analyze premium performance, revenue opportunities, renewals, and cross-sell metrics for data-driven business decisions.

## Key Analysis Performed
- Premium Amount Analysis
- Meetings Held by Year
- Stage-wise Revenue Analysis
- Opportunities by Revenue
- Cross-Sell Performance Tracking
- Renewal vs New Business Target Analysis

## Tools Used
- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Tableau

## Skills Demonstrated
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- KPI Reporting
- Sales & Revenue Analytics
- Dashboard Development
- Business Intelligence

## Key Outcomes
- Analyzed revenue opportunities across sales stages
- Tracked premium growth and business targets
- Evaluated cross-sell and renewal performance
- Generated insights to support sales strategy and revenue optimization
